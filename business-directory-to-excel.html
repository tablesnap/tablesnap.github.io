From a list of names to a contact sheet

Directories such as yellow pages, chambers of commerce, trade associations and marketplaces list hundreds of companies, but rarely let you download them. Copying them one by one takes hours.

TableSnap extracts the whole list in one click. Then “Find emails & phones” opens each company's website in the background, reads its home and contact pages, and adds what it finds as new columns.

Step by step

  1. Open the directorySearch the directory for the sector and area you need, for example “plumbers in Barcelona”.
  2. Pick the listOpen TableSnap (Alt+Shift+T), press “Pick table or list on page” and click the list of companies. Each company becomes a row, with its website as a link column.
  3. Collect every pagePress “Auto-paginate” to follow the directory's “Next” button. Duplicate companies are skipped automatically.
  4. Find emails & phonesUnder “Find emails & phones”, press “Enrich from links”. TableSnap visits each website listed in your table and adds the public emails, phone numbers and social profiles it finds.
  5. Download ExcelPress “Download Excel”. Every company stays on one row, with its contact details in their own columns.

Use it responsibly. TableSnap only collects contact details that companies publish on their own websites. Before you contact anyone, check the data-protection rules that apply to you (such as the GDPR in Europe) and never send unsolicited bulk messages. See our Terms.

Tips

  • Small businesses often publish only a phone number. That's normal: TableSnap leaves the email cell empty instead of inventing one.
  • It also reads emails hidden from bots, WhatsApp links and the contact page, so it finds more than a quick look at the home page.
  • The free plan lets you try the search on 3 websites; Pro checks every row in your table.
  • Your browser visits the websites directly and without cookies. Nothing goes through our servers.

Questions

Which directories does it work with?

Most online directories and listings that show companies as a list: yellow pages, association member lists, marketplaces, event exhibitor lists and similar. If the directory links to each company's website, TableSnap can look up their contact details.

Where do the emails and phone numbers come from?

Only from the public websites listed in your table. TableSnap opens each one in your own browser and reads the home page and, if needed, the contact page. It doesn't use any database or third-party service.

Is it free?

Extracting and downloading the directory is free with no row limit. Finding emails and phones is free on 3 websites so you can try it; Pro ($9/month or $49/year) does it for every row.

Why are some emails missing?

Many small businesses only publish a phone number or a contact form. TableSnap leaves the cell empty rather than guessing an address.

Is collecting this data legal?

It depends on where you are and how you use it. Company contact details published on a business website are generally public, but data-protection laws such as the GDPR still apply to how you store and use them. When in doubt, ask a professional.
