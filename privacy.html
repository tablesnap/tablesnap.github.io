Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TableSnap – Web Table & List Extractor Chrome extension (“TableSnap”, “the extension”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The short version: TableSnap works entirely inside your own browser. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share the data you extract.

1. What TableSnap does

TableSnap lets you select a table or a repeating list on the web page you are viewing, preview it in Chrome's side panel, and export it (copy to Excel/Google Sheets, download CSV, copy as Markdown/JSON for AI assistants, or send it to a webhook you choose). Optionally, it can visit the links contained in your extracted list to find public contact details (emails, phone numbers, social profile links and page titles).

2. Data processed locally on your device

All of the following happens only on your computer, inside your Chrome browser:

  • Page content you select. When you click a table or list, the extension reads that part of the page to build rows and columns. This happens only after you start the picker, and only on the tab you are using.
  • Link enrichment. When you click “Enrich from Links”, your browser downloads the public web pages listed in your table and parses them locally to find emails, phone numbers, social links and page titles. These requests are sent directly from your browser to those websites, without cookies (credentials: omit). The results are never sent to us.
  • Exports. Copying to the clipboard and downloading CSV files are performed locally by your browser.

We have no servers that receive your extracted data, and we cannot see it.

3. Data stored on your device (chrome.storage)

TableSnap uses Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), which stays on your device, to remember:

ItemPurpose
Your last extracted tableSo you don't lose your work if you close and reopen the side panel. You can delete it at any time with the “Clear” button.
Pro license key, activation ID, status and last validation timeTo keep TableSnap Pro active on this device.
Your webhook URL (if you use webhooks)So you don't have to type it again.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of this data from your browser.

4. Network connections the extension makes

TableSnap only connects to the internet in these situations:

  • Polar.sh license server (api.polar.sh) – only if you enter a Pro license key. The extension sends your license key, our organization ID and an activation ID to Polar's public customer-portal API to activate, validate (at most about once every 12 hours) or deactivate your license. No extracted data and no browsing data is sent. Polar.sh is our payment and licensing provider (Merchant of Record); purchases are handled on Polar's checkout pages under Polar's Privacy Policy.
  • The webhook URL you enter – only when you click “Send”. Your exported rows are sent directly from your browser to that address (for example your own n8n, Make or Zapier workflow). We do not receive a copy.
  • The links in your table – only when you click “Enrich from Links”, as described in section 2.

TableSnap does not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs or remote code. All code is included in the extension package.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect your browsing history.
  • We do not collect or store the content of the pages you visit or the data you extract.
  • We do not sell, rent or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

The use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Payment information

Payments are processed by Polar.sh. We never see or store your card details. Polar sends us the information needed to manage your subscription (such as your email address and license status) under their own privacy policy.

8. Your responsibilities

You decide which pages you extract and what you do with the results. Please respect websites' terms of service and applicable data-protection laws (such as the GDPR) when you collect or use contact information.

9. Children

TableSnap is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the extension's store listing.
