Too many tabs, too many hotels

Planning a trip or a work event usually ends with twenty open tabs and a notebook full of prices. Booking.com lets you sort, but not compare price against score and location side by side.

With TableSnap the search results become a table. Each hotel is a row, and the area, score, number of reviews and price for your dates sit in their own columns.

Step by step

  1. Search on Booking.comEnter your destination, dates and number of guests. Apply the filters you need (price, stars, free cancellation) so the list only has hotels you would book.
  2. Open TableSnapClick the TableSnap icon in Chrome's toolbar or press Alt+Shift+T. The side panel opens on the right.
  3. Click the hotel listPress “Pick table or list on page”, move the mouse over the list of hotels until it is outlined in green, and click.
  4. Load more hotelsBooking.com adds hotels as you scroll or when you press its “Load more results” button. Press “Auto-paginate” and TableSnap does that for you, without duplicates.
  5. Download ExcelPress “Download Excel”, or use “Copy for Google Sheets / Excel” to share the list with the people you travel with.

What you get

One row per hotel. The columns depend on what Booking.com shows for your search. Usually they are:

Good to know: prices on Booking.com depend on your dates, guests and sometimes your country or account. Extract the list after setting all of them, and check the final price on Booking.com before you book.

Tips

  • Booking.com usually shows the price for the whole stay. Divide it by the number of nights to compare price per night.
  • Sort by Score and then filter by Price to find the best-rated hotels in your budget.
  • Hide columns you don't need under “Columns” before downloading – hidden columns are left out of the file.
  • Use “Copy for AI” and ask your AI assistant to shortlist hotels near a specific address.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Extracting and downloading is free with no row limit. The free plan loads up to 3 more batches of hotels automatically; Pro loads up to 100.

Does it work with Airbnb or other travel sites?

Yes. TableSnap works on any site that shows a list, so you can do the same with other booking and travel websites.

Why do some hotels have an empty price?

Booking.com hides the price when a hotel has no rooms for your dates or guests. Those rows keep the hotel's other details.

Do I need to sign in to Booking.com?

No. If you are signed in, TableSnap reads the prices you see, including any member discounts.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your Chrome browser. Nothing you extract reaches our servers.
