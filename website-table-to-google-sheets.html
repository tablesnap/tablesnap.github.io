When IMPORTHTML is not enough

Google Sheets has a formula for this: =IMPORTHTML("url", "table", 1). It works on simple public pages, but it often fails: “Could not fetch url”, an empty result, or the wrong table because you have to guess its number.

The reason is that Google's servers download the page, not your browser. They can't see pages that need a login, content that loads with JavaScript, or lists made of cards instead of a real HTML table. TableSnap reads the page in your own browser, exactly as you see it, so those problems go away.

Step by step

  1. Open the page in ChromeGo to the page with the table you want. If it needs a login, sign in as usual.
  2. Open TableSnapClick the TableSnap icon in Chrome's toolbar or press Alt+Shift+T. The side panel opens on the right.
  3. Click the tablePress “Pick table or list on page”, point at the table until it is outlined in green, and click. No need to count tables or write formulas.
  4. Copy itPress “Copy for Google Sheets / Excel”. The rows are copied with one value per cell.
  5. Paste into Google SheetsClick cell A1 of your sheet and press Ctrl+V (⌘+V on Mac). Or press “Download Excel” and open the file in Google Sheets with File → Import.

Numbers stay numbers. TableSnap removes footnote marks like [12] and thousand separators before copying, so SUM, charts and sorting work as soon as you paste.

Tips

  • Paste into an empty sheet: the first row holds the column names.
  • Click a column title in the side panel to rename it before you copy.
  • Hide the columns you don't need under “Columns” – they are left out of the copy.
  • For a list spread over several pages, press “Auto-paginate” first and copy everything at once.

Questions

Why does IMPORTHTML say “Could not fetch url”?

Google's servers could not download the page – often because the site blocks them, needs a login, or builds the table with JavaScript. Reading the page in your own browser with TableSnap avoids all three.

Does the sheet update automatically?

No. TableSnap copies the data as it is right now. Run it again when you need fresh data, or use IMPORTHTML for simple public pages that you want to refresh on their own.

Is there a row limit?

No. The free plan copies any number of rows.

Do I need a Google account connection or permissions?

No. TableSnap never connects to your Google account. It copies the rows to your clipboard and you paste them.

Does it work with Excel too?

Yes. The same button pastes into Excel, and “Download Excel” creates a real .xlsx file.
