Comparing prices by hand takes forever

An Amazon search shows dozens of similar products, each with its own price, stars and review count. Comparing them means scrolling up and down and writing things down.

TableSnap turns the results page into a table. Each product becomes a row with its price, rating and reviews in separate columns, so you can sort by price or by rating in Excel in seconds.

Step by step

  1. Search on AmazonOpen Amazon in your country and search for the product, for example “wireless headphones”. Use Amazon's own filters (brand, price range) first if you want a shorter list.
  2. Open TableSnapClick the TableSnap icon in Chrome's toolbar or press Alt+Shift+T. The side panel opens on the right.
  3. Click the resultsPress “Pick table or list on page”, move the mouse over the list of products until it is outlined in green, and click. The products appear in the side panel.
  4. Add the next pagesPress “Auto-paginate”. TableSnap clicks Amazon's “Next” button for you and adds each page, without duplicate products. Press “Stop” whenever you have enough.
  5. Download ExcelPress “Download Excel”. Each product is one row, with its price, rating and number of reviews in separate columns.

What you get

One row per product. The columns depend on what Amazon shows for your search. Usually they are:

Good to know: Amazon prices change often and can depend on your location and delivery address. Write down the date of each export, and use the data for your own research – TableSnap only reads what you can already see on the page.

Tips for price research

  • Sponsored products appear mixed with the normal results. Add a column in Excel to mark them if you want to compare like with like.
  • Export the same search every week and paste it into a new sheet: you get a simple price history.
  • Use “Copy for AI” and ask ChatGPT or Claude which products give the best value for the price.
  • If Amazon shows prices with and without VAT (for example on a business account), you get two price columns. Keep the one you need under “Columns”.

Questions

Is it free to export Amazon results to Excel?

Yes. Extracting and downloading is free with no row limit. The free plan adds up to 3 result pages automatically; Pro adds up to 100.

Does it work on every Amazon country site?

Yes. TableSnap reads the page you are viewing, so it works on Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.es, Amazon.de and the rest. Prices keep the currency format of each site.

Can I export a single product page?

TableSnap is made for lists. On a product page you can still pick any table on it, such as the technical details or a comparison chart.

Do I need an Amazon account or an API key?

No. Nothing to sign up for. TableSnap works in your own browser on the pages you open.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your Chrome browser. The products you extract never reach our servers.
