How it works

Three steps. No settings.

  1. 1

    Open the panel

    Click the TableSnap icon in Chrome (or press Alt+Shift+T).

  2. 2

    Click the data

    Hover the table or list on the page and click once. Every row appears instantly.

  3. 3

    Take it anywhere

    Excel, Google Sheets, CSV, your AI assistant or your automations.

Private by design

There is no TableSnap server collecting what you extract. The extension reads the page you choose, on your computer, and that's it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Write to patatapy@gmail.com – a real person replies.

Do you see or store the data I extract?

No. Extraction and the email & phone search run entirely inside your Chrome browser. Your tables never reach our servers. Read our Privacy Policy.

Which websites does it work on?

Most websites with tables or repeating lists: maps, directories, marketplaces, search results, Wikipedia, dashboards and more. It can't run on Chrome's own pages or the Chrome Web Store.

Is the free plan really unlimited?

Yes. There's no row limit on extracting, downloading or copying. Pro adds the email & phone search for every row, long pagination and webhooks.

How do I activate Pro?

Buy a plan, copy the license key from your Polar email, open TableSnap, click the “Free Plan” badge and paste the key.

Can I cancel or move my license?

Cancel anytime from the Polar link in your receipt. To move to another computer, click the “Pro Plan” badge, choose “Deactivate this device” and activate the key on the new one.
