Questions

Do you see or store the data I extract?

No. Extraction and enrichment run entirely inside your Chrome browser. Your tables never reach our servers. Read our Privacy Policy.

Which websites does it work on?

Most websites with tables or repeating lists: directories, marketplaces, search results, Wikipedia, dashboards and more. It can't run on Chrome's own pages or the Chrome Web Store.

How do I activate Pro?

Buy a plan, copy the license key from your Polar email, open TableSnap, click the “Free Plan” badge and paste the key. That's it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the Polar customer portal link in your receipt email.

Can I move my license to another computer?

Yes. Click the “Pro Plan” badge and choose “Deactivate this device”, then activate the same key on the new computer.
