Why copy-paste doesn't work on Maps

Google Maps has no export button, and selecting the results list with the mouse gives you one long block of text: names, stars, opening hours and addresses all mixed together.

TableSnap reads the list the way you see it. Each place becomes a row and each piece of information gets its own column, so the file opens in Excel ready to sort and filter.

Step by step

  1. Search on Google MapsOpen google.com/maps and search for what you need, for example “dentists in Valencia” or “coffee shops near me”. Wait for the results list on the left.
  2. Open TableSnapClick the TableSnap icon in Chrome's toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+T). The side panel opens next to the map.
  3. Click the results listPress “Pick table or list on page”, move the mouse over the list of places until it is outlined in green, and click. The rows appear in the side panel.
  4. Load more placesMaps only shows about 20 places at first. Press “Auto-paginate” and TableSnap scrolls the list for you and adds the new places without duplicates.
  5. Download ExcelPress “Download Excel”. You get a real .xlsx file with one place per row. You can also download CSV or copy everything straight into Google Sheets.

What you get

Every place in the list becomes one row. The columns depend on what Maps shows for your search. Usually they are:

Good to know: the results list doesn't include phone numbers or websites – Maps only shows them when you open each place. If you need contact details, extract a business directory instead and use “Find emails & phones” (see the guide below).

Tips for cleaner data

  • Zoom the map to the area you care about before extracting: Maps only lists places inside the visible area.
  • Maps often puts a few sponsored places at the top of the list. Delete those rows in Excel if you don't need them.
  • Click a column title in the side panel to rename it before you download.
  • Hide the columns you don't need under “Columns”. Hidden columns are left out of the file.

Questions

Is it free to export Google Maps to Excel with TableSnap?

Yes. Extracting and downloading is free with no row limit. The free plan loads up to 3 pages of results automatically; Pro loads up to 100 pages.

How many places can I export?

As many as Google Maps shows for your search. Maps stops listing new places after roughly 120 results per search, so for bigger areas run several searches (for example one per neighbourhood).

Can I get phone numbers and emails from Google Maps?

Not from the results list, because Maps doesn't show them there. Business directories usually do list each company's website, and TableSnap Pro can visit those websites and collect the public emails and phone numbers.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Use “Copy for Google Sheets / Excel” and paste into any sheet, or download the .xlsx file and open it with Google Sheets.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. TableSnap runs inside your Chrome browser. The places you extract never reach our servers.
